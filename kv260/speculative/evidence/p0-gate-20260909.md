## Handoff rule

P0 artifacts are frozen. P2 and later stages must reuse the existing XSA,
bitstream, PS initialization, BSP, and ELF unless a stage explicitly changes the
hardware interface. Full-system rebuilds are not part of routine P2 iteration;
P2 uses focused module generation, simulation, and out-of-context synthesis.
